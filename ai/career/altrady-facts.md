# Altrady engagement — facts only (as of 2026-08-25)

Neutral data sheet for obtaining an independent opinion on rate and terms.
Contains no recommendations or target numbers.

## Engagement

- Areg Shahbazian, freelance frontend software engineer.
- Client: Altrady (crypto trading platform; Altrady BV, Den Haag, NL; invoiced
  via Web2000 BV until Apr 2021). Small team, roughly 10–20 people.
- Engaged continuously since **September 2018** (~8 years). Hourly rate,
  invoiced in 160-hour blocks. No equity, no benefits, no notice period
  beyond freelance norms.
- Contracting setup: Areg is an unincorporated individual invoicing from
  abroad; invoices carry no VAT ("VAT reverse charged") and no tax ID. For the
  client this is a plain deductible foreign invoice: no VAT to pre-finance, no
  employer contributions, no payroll or Dutch contractor-compliance overhead.
  The €25/hr is the full cost to the client.
- Fully remote from a different timezone; flexible hours; vacations taken as
  scattered days rather than blocks.
- Background: Armenian, immigrated to the Netherlands at a young age; raised and
  educated in NL, fluent Dutch, Dutch work culture. Lives mostly in Armenia and
  the Philippines. I.e. not an offshore hire in the usual sense — a
  Dutch-trained engineer working remotely from abroad.
- Owner/CEO (Benoist Claassen) is a software engineer and actively commits
  code. Other contributor: UI designer (Roman Ivanov), at the company since 2018.
- Current assignment: integration of a new charting engine ("Superchart")
  replacing the previous TradingView integration in the desktop app; started
  early 2026, planned delivery ~November 2026.
