## Rest of the shopping list

- **Coloured heatshrink** — white and yellow are essential (the ring tracers for
  `B/W` and `O/Y`). Others optional.
- **Ratcheting open-barrel crimper** — non-negotiable for repeatable crimps.
- **Spare terminals + seals** — the first few crimps go in the bin by design.
- **Loom tape / split conduit** for rewrapping.
- **Connector:** matched aftermarket 6-pin pair to replace the Sumitomo HM-series green
  original. Keep the old halves as the pinout reference. Verify 1.5 mm² fits the wire
  barrels before committing.

## Salvage option

Using wire from the stash instead of buying is acceptable — the acceptance criteria
(multi-strand, cross-section, insulation temperature rating) are in
`07-ignition-switch-harness.md`.
